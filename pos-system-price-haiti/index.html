What many businesses in Haiti deal with:

  • ✗ Prices you only learn after three meetings.
  • ✗ A cheap system with no one in Haiti to set it up or answer your questions.
  • ✗ Paying for features you'll never use.

Two parts: the software and the machine.

The BPRO POS software is a yearly subscription in gourdes. START (15,000 HTG a year) covers one location and up to 5 users. BUSINESS (50,000 HTG) adds several locations, more users and advanced reports. CORPORATE (250,000 HTG) is for companies that need unlimited branches, a website and integrations.

The POS machine is optional and paid once, from $250 for the Mobile 7 to $500 for the Restaurant Mobile. If you already have a computer, a tablet or a printer, you only pay for the software. The final price depends on your business, your users, your locations and the modules you need.

What every plan includes.

Setup and configuration

Training

Your team learns to sell, and you learn to read your reports. There are videos for every menu.

Support in Kreyòl and French

Pay the subscription by MonCash, NatCash or bank transfer. We activate your account after the payment.

Questions about POS prices.

Is there a free POS system?

You can look around BPRO for free on demo.pointofsales.fr, with a demo account for each type of business. To work with your own data, your business needs its own paid account.

How often do I pay?

The software is paid once a year. The POS machine is paid once.

Can I start small and upgrade later?

Yes. Start with what you need and add modules as your business grows. The BUSINESS plan adds several locations and more than 5 users.

How do I pay?

By MonCash, NatCash or bank transfer, on pay.pointofsales.fr.

Are setup and training included?

Yes. Every plan includes setup, configuration, training and support. We stay available on WhatsApp.
