Pharmacy POS system in Haiti

What many businesses in Haiti deal with:

  • ✗ You lose time looking for a medicine while the customer waits.
  • ✗ Products expire on the shelves and nobody sees it.
  • ✗ You order by guesswork because nobody knows the real stock.

The register, the stock and the suppliers in one system.

At the register, you search a medicine by its name, its code or its barcode, take the payment in cash, by card, MonCash or NatCash, and the receipt prints. The stock goes down with every sale.

To run the pharmacy, BPRO follows the quantities, warns you when a product is about to run out, and keeps track of purchases and what you owe suppliers. Every day you see what sells, what is left and what you earn, even when you're not at the pharmacy.

Key features.

Start with the essentials and add more as your business grows.

Fast search

Find a product by name, SKU or barcode right in the register.

Expiry dates

Track the expiry dates of your products so you can sell before it's too late.

Stock alerts

Each product has an alert quantity; the dashboard shows what is about to run out.

Purchases and suppliers

Record purchases, payments to suppliers and what you owe them.

Invoices and receipts

A printed receipt for every sale, and invoices for customers who ask.

Employees and access

Each cashier has their own account; the activity log shows who did what.

Pharmacy stock control

Adjust the stock when a product is broken or expired, transfer products between two pharmacies, and print barcode labels.

Some modules and features depend on your plan, your equipment and how your business is set up.

Questions about pharmacy POS.

Is BPRO complete pharmacy management software?

Yes. BPRO handles the register (POS), stock, purchases, suppliers, invoices, employees and reports on sales and profit.

Can I track the expiry dates of medicines?

Yes, BPRO tracks the expiry dates of your products to help you sell before they expire.

What equipment does a pharmacy need?

A compatible computer or tablet is enough. We also offer the POS Fixe 15.6" and the Mobile 7 with a built-in printer.

Can my employees steal without me seeing it?

Each employee has their own access, every action is recorded in the activity log, and the register report shows each user.

How much does BPRO POS cost in Haiti?

The software costs 15,000 HTG a year (START), 50,000 HTG (BUSINESS), 250,000 HTG (CORPORATE) or 25,000 HTG (EDUCATION). POS machines are optional and paid once, from $250 to $500. The final price depends on your users, locations and modules.

Are setup and training included?

Yes. Every plan includes setup, configuration, training and support. We stay available on WhatsApp.

Can my customers pay with MonCash and NatCash?

Yes. MonCash and NatCash are payment methods in BPRO, along with cash, card, cheque and bank transfer.
