How the BPRO cash register works.

Each day starts with opening the register. The cashier counts the starting cash in the drawer and types it in. From then on, every sale is recorded with its payment method: cash, card, MonCash, NatCash, cheque or transfer.

At closing time, BPRO shows the cash expected in the drawer: the starting cash plus the cash sales of the day. The cashier counts the drawer, types the total, and closes the register. If money is missing, the gap shows up in the register report, by cashier.

Questions about cash registers.

Can I use a barcode scanner with BPRO?

Yes. A USB scanner works like a keyboard, so there is usually nothing to install. Click in the search box and scan.

Does the cash drawer open by itself?

Most cash drawers plug into the receipt printer or into a terminal with a drawer port, like the POS Fixe or the Tablet 11", and open when the receipt prints. If yours doesn't, check the cable and the printer settings, or write to us on WhatsApp.

How do I know if a cashier is short?

When the cashier closes the register, BPRO compares the counted cash with the expected cash. The gap appears in the register report, with the cashier's name.

Can I have several cash registers?

Yes. Each cashier has their own account and opens their own register. Everything comes together in the same reports.

Can my customers pay with MonCash and NatCash?

Yes. MonCash and NatCash are payment methods in BPRO, along with cash, card, cheque and bank transfer.
