What is a POS system?

A POS (point of sale) system is the software and the machine you use at the counter to record each sale. You scan or pick the product, take the payment and print the receipt. Behind the counter, it keeps track of what you sold, what is left in stock and how much money should be in the cash drawer.

BPRO POS does all of that and connects the rest of your business: purchases from suppliers, customer credit, expenses, employees and reports.

Questions about POS systems in Haiti.

How much does a POS system cost in Haiti?

With BPRO POS, the software costs 15,000 HTG a year (START), 50,000 HTG (BUSINESS), 250,000 HTG (CORPORATE) or 25,000 HTG (EDUCATION). POS machines are optional and paid once, from $250 (Mobile 7) to $500 (Restaurant kit). The final price depends on your users, locations and modules.

Do I need to buy a POS machine?

No, if you already have a computer, a tablet, a printer or a compatible terminal. If you don't, take a BPRO kit.

Does BPRO POS work with a barcode scanner?

Yes. A USB scanner works like a keyboard: put the cursor in the search box, scan, and the product goes into the cart. If a code isn't in your stock yet, an administrator can add the product or link the code to a product you already have.

Can my customers pay with MonCash and NatCash?

Yes. MonCash and NatCash are payment methods in BPRO, along with cash, card, cheque and bank transfer. You can also split one sale between two methods.

Can I use BPRO POS in several stores?

Yes. The BUSINESS and CORPORATE plans follow sales, stock and teams across your locations, with stock transfers between them.

Are setup and training included?

Yes. Every plan includes setup, configuration, training and support. We stay available on WhatsApp.

Can I see BPRO POS before I buy?

Yes. demo.pointofsales.fr has a free demo account for each type of business, just for looking around. Your business then works in its own account.

How do I pay for the subscription?

By MonCash, NatCash or bank transfer. We activate your account after the payment.
